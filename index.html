Module 01 • Trajectory Mapping

Shopper Flow & Path Tracking

Tracks customer paths across store concourses and grocery aisles. Identifies whether shoppers take perimeter loops or navigate through center shelves.

Dwell-Time Heatmaps

Builds persistent thermal density maps showing where customers linger to inspect product labels versus cold dead-zones that shoppers quickly bypass.

High Dwell (> 4m)

ACADEMIC RESEARCH & PROJECT TEAM

About The Project & Engineering Team

RetailFlow is a computer vision engineering capstone developed to bring non-invasive footfall tracking and spatial density analytics to modern supermarket layouts.

Faculty Guidance & Mentorship Project Mentor
VS

Prof. Venkata Subramanian J

Project Guide & Technical Mentor

Technical Guidance & Architectural Leadership

Under the technical mentorship of Prof. Venkata Subramanian J, the project team designed, developed, and experimentally validated each stage of the computer vision pipeline:

  • Pipeline Architecture & Model Selection: Guided the comparative evaluation of object detection models, leading to the selection of lightweight YOLOv8 models paired with centroid-based tracking and Gaussian spatial accumulation for accurate 2D dwell heatmaps.
  • Real-Time Edge Architecture: Steered the technical design away from latency-heavy cloud dependencies toward efficient on-premise edge computing, enabling real-time frame processing with minimal computational overhead.
  • Privacy-First Ethical Standards: Enforced strict privacy-by-design compliance, ensuring the system operates without facial recognition or biometric identification, relying purely on anonymized centroid coordinates.
  • Milestone Reviews & Verification: Provided continuous academic guidance through code reviews, metric verification, synthetic and real video testing, and structured executive PDF performance report generation.

System Architecture Pipeline

Stage 01

CCTV Video Ingestion

Connects to standard supermarket IP/RTSP camera feeds across aisles, coolers, and registers.

Stage 02

YOLOv8 Detection & Tracking

Identifies shoppers and tracks centroid movement vectors with low-latency edge inference.

Stage 03

Gaussian Heatmap Accumulation

Accumulates footstep coordinates into 2D density matrices to highlight hot and cold shelves.

Stage 04

Operational Store Analytics

Delivers live floor dashboards, queue alerts, and automated PDF summaries for store management.
